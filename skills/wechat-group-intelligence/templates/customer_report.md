# 客户服务群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `customer`. Do not infer satisfaction from politeness or silence.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 客户状态

#### 4.1.1 服务事项、参与人员、进度与态度

Separate observed service status from inferred attitude; cite recent and contradictory signals.

### 4.2 客户需求

#### 4.2.1 已确认、新增、变化与未满足需求

Show prior/current statements and whether the service side acknowledged the change.

### 4.3 满意度与反馈

#### 4.3.1 正向、中性和负向反馈

Report evidence distribution, not an invented satisfaction percentage. Preserve critical customer quotes.

### 4.4 服务问题与风险

#### 4.4.1 响应、交付、质量、关系与续约风险

For each, include severity, evidence, owner, missing facts, and recovery action.

### 4.5 增购与续约机会

#### 4.5.1 新需求、追加服务、转介绍与长期合作

Keep service recovery separate from commercial follow-up; a complaint is not automatically an upsell opportunity.

### 4.6 客户行动建议

#### 4.6.1 处理顺序

Prioritize immediate service recovery, proactive clarification, delivery improvement, relationship maintenance, then consent-based commercial follow-up.
