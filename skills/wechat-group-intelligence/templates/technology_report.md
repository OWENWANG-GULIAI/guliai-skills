# 技术交流群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `technology`. Keep claims, hypotheses, attempted fixes, reproduced results, and verified solutions distinct.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 技术主题概览

#### 4.1.1 高频与新出现主题

List topic, participant count, message evidence, and whether “new” can be established from the available time range.

#### 4.1.2 热度变化

Only claim rise or decline when comparable time windows exist.

### 4.2 核心知识与方法

#### 4.2.1 原理、操作、最佳实践与常见错误

Attach speaker/source, applicability, limitations, and verification status.

### 4.3 问题与解决方案

#### 4.3.1 高频问题

Group equivalent symptoms without merging materially different environments.

#### 4.3.2 方案状态

Separate `已验证`, `有人声称有效`, `尝试失败`, `待测试`, and `未解决`.

### 4.4 趋势与应用机会

#### 4.4.1 工具、能力、应用趋势与风险

Do not convert group opinion into market fact without outside evidence.

#### 4.4.2 用户应用

Propose only profile-grounded business use, course/Skill ideas, knowledge cards, and content topics.

### 4.5 关键专家与发言

#### 4.5.1 专业贡献者

Rank by useful, evidenced contribution and successful problem solving—not message count.
