# 销售成交群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `sales`. Purchase signals do not prove authority or commitment; explicit refusal and stop-contact requests override follow-up enthusiasm.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 线索与需求

#### 4.1.1 线索状态

Separate new, active, dormant, and loss-risk leads using visible interactions and time coverage.

#### 4.1.2 明确需求、隐含需求、预算与采购时间

Mark hidden needs as inference. Preserve negation and conditions in quoted evidence.

### 4.2 成交阶段

#### 4.2.1 阶段判断

Choose only one best-supported stage: initial understanding, needs confirmation, solution discussion, quote/negotiation, pending signature, won, or lost. Give evidence and confidence.

### 4.3 决策链

#### 4.3.1 需求提出者、使用者、影响者、决策者与付款/采购角色

Unknown roles remain unknown. “我要问老板” suggests a contact or possible influencer, not a confirmed decision-maker.

### 4.4 障碍与机会

#### 4.4.1 障碍

Classify price, trust, unclear need, solution mismatch, decision cycle, competitor, and internal approval only when supported.

#### 4.4.2 机会等级

Show evidence, missing prerequisites, value score, and what would change the score.

### 4.5 跟进建议

#### 4.5.1 人工审阅的下一步

Each suggestion includes contact, reason, topic, material, timing, objective, source evidence, and whether the customer consented to further contact. Never send automatically.
