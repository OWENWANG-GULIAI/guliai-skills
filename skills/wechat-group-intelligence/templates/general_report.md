# 综合关系群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when no specific scene reaches the evidence threshold, the group is genuinely mixed-purpose, or the input is too limited. This is a confidence-preserving fallback, not a weaker excuse to invent business value.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 群聊用途与证据限制

#### 4.1.1 可观察用途

Describe supported topics and interaction patterns without forcing a single business label.

#### 4.1.2 为什么未进入专项场景

Show candidate scene signals, missing dimensions, and confidence.

### 4.2 有价值信息

#### 4.2.1 事实、问题、决策、任务与知识

Include only items that meet the normal evidence and value contract.

### 4.3 重要关系

#### 4.3.1 专业、商业、内容与协作关系候选

Use `候选` or `待确认` unless repeated interaction supports a stronger label.

### 4.4 关系维护与行动

#### 4.4.1 建议联系对象、理由和方式

Recommend contact only when useful and appropriate; ordinary social chat may require no action.

### 4.5 关键发言沉淀

#### 4.5.1 原话与可复用价值

Preserve provenance and explicitly state when no high-value material is present.
