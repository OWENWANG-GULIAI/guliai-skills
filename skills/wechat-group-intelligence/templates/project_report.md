# 项目协作群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `project`. A proposed task is not an accepted assignment until ownership is evidenced.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 项目概览

#### 4.1.1 目标、阶段、里程碑与整体状态

Label every inferred project state and cite the supporting updates.

### 4.2 任务分析

#### 4.2.1 已确认任务

|任务|负责人|截止时间|状态|证据|确定性|
|-|-|-|-|-|-|

#### 4.2.2 信息缺口

List tasks missing owner, deadline, deliverable, acceptance condition, or confirmed status.

#### 4.2.3 逾期或停滞

Require a promised date plus current evidence before calling a task overdue.

### 4.3 决策与变更

#### 4.3.1 已确认、待确认、已变化的决策

Show earlier and later evidence, who confirmed it, and the impact.

### 4.4 风险

#### 4.4.1 进度、资源、质量、沟通与客户风险

For each risk include evidence, likelihood/confidence, impact, owner, mitigation, and escalation condition.

### 4.5 协作与行动

#### 4.5.1 负责人、执行者与协作断点

Infer roles only from task assignment, reporting, approval, or other interaction evidence.

#### 4.5.2 下一步

Split actions into 24 hours, this week, and before the next milestone.
