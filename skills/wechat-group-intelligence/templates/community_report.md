# 社群运营群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `community`. Visible participation is not the same as readership, satisfaction, or retention.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 社群健康度

#### 4.1.1 活跃度与参与层级

Report speakers, interactions, and time coverage. Classify visible core, active, and low-visible-participation users with evidence.

### 4.2 用户需求

#### 4.2.1 高频主题、关注变化与痛点

Separate work, learning, and business pain points; count independent speakers where possible.

### 4.3 用户画像

#### 4.3.1 可见类型与高价值用户

Do not infer profession or purchase power without evidence. Identify potential customers, opinion leaders, and sharing nodes as hypotheses with confidence.

### 4.4 运营优化

#### 4.4.1 内容、活动与转化

For each recommendation include evidence, target segment, experiment, success signal, and review date.

### 4.5 商业机会

#### 4.5.1 购买信号与成长路径

Keep inquiry, intent, and purchase distinct. Do not force commercial interpretation onto ordinary interactions.

### 4.6 关键人物与用户声音

#### 4.6.1 人物和原话

Preserve representative positive, negative, and unresolved voices rather than selecting only praise.
