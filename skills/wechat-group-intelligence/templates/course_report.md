# 课程学习群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `course`. Do not infer silent members' learning status; “旁观” only means no visible participation in the supplied records.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 参与情况

#### 4.1.1 可见参与指标

Report speakers, questions, assignments/check-ins, and active periods only when the input supports counting. State the denominator and coverage.

#### 4.1.2 参与层级

Classify evidence-backed core learners, active learners, low-visible-participation members, and possible disengagement risks. Give evidence and confidence per person.

### 4.2 学习效果

#### 4.2.1 高频收获与已产生结果

Separate self-reported gains, observable work products, and independently verified results.

#### 4.2.2 高频问题与迁移障碍

Group by comprehension, operation, workplace transfer, missing examples, and missing feedback.

### 4.3 课程评价与产品优化

#### 4.3.1 高认可内容、低理解内容与内容缺口

For each item include who said what and how often independent speakers expressed it.

#### 4.3.2 优化建议

Separate content, teaching method, assignment design, and coaching/feedback improvements.

### 4.4 高价值学员与真实声音

#### 4.4.1 人物类型

Identify evidence-backed case candidates, possible advanced learners, sharing/referral nodes, and people needing help.

#### 4.4.2 原话

Preserve exact quotes for gains, difficulties, suggestions, and results; label excerpts.
