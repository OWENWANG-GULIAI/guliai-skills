# 行业交流与专业社群专项模板

Use inside `四、场景专项分析` when the primary scene is `industry`. Distinguish group statements from externally verified market facts.

## 四、场景专项分析

### 4.1 行业热点

#### 4.1.1 高频议题、新趋势、争议观点与事件

State the covered period and participant breadth. “Trend” requires comparison; otherwise call it a prominent topic in this sample.

### 4.2 专业知识

#### 4.2.1 方法、案例、数据与可验证观点

Attach source person/message, stated source URL or publication when present, applicability, and verification status.

### 4.3 资源与合作

#### 4.3.1 项目、客户、专家、平台和渠道资源

Do not expose private contact details unless necessary and authorized.

#### 4.3.2 联合项目、内容、课程和资源互换机会

Separate offered cooperation from speculative fit.

### 4.4 关键人物

#### 4.4.1 专家、连接者、潜在客户与合作伙伴

Use interaction and contribution evidence; titles alone are insufficient.

### 4.5 对用户的启发

#### 4.5.1 应用、联系、开发与沉淀

Tie each proposal to the user's profile or mark the relevance as awaiting profile input.
